The Challenge

Bringing medical preparation into one focused experience

Medical students often move between different resources for learning, practice, revision and performance tracking.

The challenge was to design a structured experience where students can discover relevant learning content, follow a study path, practice consistently and understand their progress without feeling overwhelmed.

Design Approach

From learning to measurable progress

  1. 01

    Learn

    Access structured medical learning content and clinical resources.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Organize preparation around a focused study plan.

  3. 03

    Practice

    Test knowledge through questions, mock tests and clinical learning.

  4. 04

    Improve

    Track performance and identify areas that need more attention.

Key Design Principles

Three principles

01

Structured learning

Organize large amounts of medical content into clear learning paths so students always know where to begin and what to do next.

02

Personalised progress

Surface study plans, learning activity and performance information in a way that makes progress easier to understand.

03

Learn + Practice together

Bring learning resources and practice experiences closer together instead of making students switch between disconnected experiences.

Outcome

The final experience brings learning, planning, practice and progress into one connected platform, making the preparation journey easier to understand and navigate.

What I learned

This project helped me explore how complex educational products can be structured without overwhelming students.

The key focus was balancing information density with clear hierarchy, helping users move from learning to practice and progress with less friction.
